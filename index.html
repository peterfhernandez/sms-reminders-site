About SMS Reminders

SMS Reminders is an Australian-based service that helps individuals and businesses send timely, automated SMS notifications and reminders to their customers, clients, or contacts.

Whether you need to reduce no-shows, follow up on appointments, or keep your audience informed, SMS Reminders delivers reliable text message communications on your behalf.

Our services include:

  • Automated appointment and event reminders
  • Scheduled SMS broadcasts to contact lists
  • Custom reminder workflows for businesses
  • Two-way SMS communication support
